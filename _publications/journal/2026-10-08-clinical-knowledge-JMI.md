This study introduces **clinician-informed, anatomy-guided attention** for video-based assessment of adult spinal deformity. Clinically relevant joints and motion patterns are encoded as attention guidance for a spatiotemporal video classifier.

The framework was evaluated on monocular gait videos from 81 patients. It achieved **71.35% accuracy** and **71.12% F1-score**, compared with 62.09% accuracy and 60.13% F1-score for the corresponding no-attention baseline. Visualization analyses also showed more clinically meaningful focus.

**Links:** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JMI.13.5.054002) · [Code](https://github.com/ChenKaiXuSan/KnowledgeGuided-ASD_PyTorch)

This is a research framework for potential screening support, not a stand-alone clinical diagnostic tool.
