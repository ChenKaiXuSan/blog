This collaborative study uses a convolutional neural network to identify video frames in which an electrosurgical instrument is actually in contact with tissue. Selecting these frames supports more accurate incision trajectory reconstruction than methods based solely on thermal centroids.

The study evaluates electric and ultrasonic surgical tools in controlled experimental conditions, demonstrating the feasibility of contact-state recognition for image-guided surgical navigation.
