**Status:** Accepted for **oral presentation** at the 2026 International ACM Workshop on Multimedia Content Analysis in Sports (MMSports), co-located with ACM Multimedia 2026.

This work examines **human-centric canonicalization and dual-view 3D pose fusion** for outdoor skiing videos. The aim is to combine independently estimated poses from moving cameras without relying on calibrated camera geometry at the fusion stage.
